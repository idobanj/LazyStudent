## Brand & Style

This design system channels an elevated university editorial feel, blending rigorous academic clarity with intimate, focused productivity. Designed for students, researchers, and self-directed learners capturing and synthesizing high-density audio lectures, the interface reduces cognitive fatigue during long study sessions while maintaining an air of scholarly prestige.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Minimalist Academic** structure with **Tactile Glass & Soft Ambient Depth**. Deep burgundy and cordial maroon evoke collegiate heritage and focused intellectual drive, while soft rose-tinted neutral surfaces and airy off-whites replace clinical grays with warm, welcoming clarity. Subtle crimson focus rings and ambient luminescent halos highlight real-time AI transcription, generating a quiet, focused environment suited for late-night libraries and high-speed lecture halls.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by deep vinous tones, balanced by breathable tinted whites and warm charcoal body text.

- **Primary (`#7A1C30`)**: Velvet Burgundy. Applied to primary call-to-actions, active recording badges, high-priority highlights, and principal navigation states.
- **Secondary (`#5C1524`)**: Deep Port. Reserved for focused text headings, pressed states, deep elevation anchor elements, and rich contrast badges.
- **Tertiary (`#9E2A42`)**: Spiced Rose. Used for subtle glowing borders, interactive audio scrubber playheads, AI insight pills, and contextual accents.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F9F6F7`) & Canvas (`#FDFBFB`)**: Off-white ceramic canvases with a hint of rose undertone, preventing the eye-strain of sterile #FFFFFF while keeping contrast well above WCAG AAA standards.
- **Surface Containers**:
  - `surface-lowest`: `#FFFFFF` (crisp elevated notes, cards)
  - `surface-low`: `#FDFBFB` (canvas background)
  - `surface-mid`: `#F4EFF0` (subtle panel divisions, disabled fields)
  - `surface-high`: `#ECE5E7` (hover states, borders, chips)
- **Text & Content**:
  - `text-primary`: `#201A1B` (warm near-black for effortless reading)
  - `text-secondary`: `#4D4447` (warm muted charcoal for metadata and timestamps)
  - `text-tertiary`: `#7C7376` (placeholders, inactive icons)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial, scholarly grace of **Newsreader** for primary titles, section headers, and key quotes with the clean, legible ergonomics of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy, notes, audio transcripts, and metadata.

- **Editorial Headlines**: Newsreader gives lecture titles and lecture notebook headings an organic, bookish gravity without appearing antiquated.
- **Note Legibility**: Plus Jakarta Sans delivers open counterforms and balanced geometric curves, making prolonged scanning of dense AI summaries effortless.
- **Audio Synchronization**: Live audio transcripts use `body-lg` with an extended `1.875rem` line height to accommodate inline timestamp badges, active word highlights, and margin annotations comfortably.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a flexible 12-column layout on desktop, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12 columns with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Max container width caps at `1440px`. Two-pane layouts prioritize the audio recorder / scrubber in a persistent contextual panel (4 columns) paired with an editorial canvas (8 columns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns with `2rem` margins. Split views collapse into tabbed segments or a vertical top-pinned mini player.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Note actions float in an accessible bottom-docked command sheet.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Built upon a strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm. Spacing within audio scrubber controls and transcript bubbles strictly obeys `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px), preventing crowded interaction targets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines warm tonal layers with diffused, rose-tinted ambient drop shadows, foregoing cold gray shadows entirely.

- **Base Layer (Canvas)**: Non-elevated surface set to `#FDFBFB`.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Transcript Tiles, Inputs)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#FDFBFB` bounded by a subtle `1px` border of `rgba(122, 28, 48, 0.08)`. Supported by a gentle shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(92, 21, 36, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(92, 21, 36, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Hovered Cards, Audio Scrubber Bar)**: Lifted surface utilizing `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(92, 21, 36, 0.08), 0px 3px 6px -2px rgba(92, 21, 36, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Command Bar)**: Lifted with `0px 16px 40px -8px rgba(92, 21, 36, 0.12), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(92, 21, 36, 0.06)`.
- **Luminescent Accent Halo**: Interactive elements such as the live audio recording pill, active AI generation indicators, and playback triggers employ a soft focus halo: `0px 0px 20px 0px rgba(158, 42, 66, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces generous, welcoming rounded shapes that balance academic precision with modern warmth.

- **Small Components (10px - 12px)**: Used for buttons, inputs, tooltips, and contextual action menus (`rounded-md`).
- **Standard Cards & Transcript Blocks (16px)**: Used for note cards, transcript containers, audio waveform modules, and floating side panels (`rounded-lg`).
- **Major Surfaces & Modals (24px)**: Used for full modal sheets, floating recording panels, and preview dialogs (`rounded-xl`).
- **Pills & Badges (9999px)**: Tags, category identifiers, playback scrubbers, and audio recording toggles always resolve to full pills, contrasting against structural rectangular cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid background in `#7A1C30` with `#FFFFFF` text. Height of 44px (touch-compliant). Border radius is 12px (`rounded-md`) or pill depending on context. Hover transitions to `#5C1524` with a subtle elevation shift. Focus state features a 2px offset ring in `#9E2A42`.
- **Secondary / Soft**: Background in `rgba(122, 28, 48, 0.06)`, text in `#7A1C30`. On hover, transitions to `rgba(122, 28, 48, 0.12)`.
- **Ghost**: Transparent background, text in `#4D4447`. Hover triggers `#F4EFF0`.
- **Audio Record Action**: Dedicated round or pill-shaped button featuring a gentle breathing glow `0px 0px 16px rgba(158, 42, 66, 0.35)` when recording is live.

### Chips & Pill Tags
- **Category & Topic Tags**: Fully rounded pills with a height of 28px. Background `#F4EFF0`, text `#5C1524`, font `label-md`.
- **AI Insight Badges**: Semi-transparent `#FFFFFF` background with a 1px border in `rgba(158, 42, 66, 0.25)` and a subtle rose dot icon.

### Cards & Transcript Modules
- Base card: `#FFFFFF` surface with `16px` border-radius, `1.5rem` internal padding, and a 1px border of `rgba(122, 28, 48, 0.08)`.
- Transcript cards highlight the active spoken phrase using a gentle tint (`rgba(158, 42, 66, 0.08)`) with an inline pill indicating the active lecture timestamp (`04:22`).

### Input Fields
- Text inputs and search bars feature `#FFFFFF` background, a 1px border in `rgba(77, 68, 71, 0.2)`, and 12px corner radius.
- Active focus transitions the border to `#7A1C30` with an ambient glow of `0px 0px 0px 3px rgba(122, 28, 48, 0.12)`. Placeholder text in `#7C7376`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px controls with 6px radius for checkboxes and full circles for radio buttons.
- Default state: 1.5px border in `rgba(77, 68, 71, 0.35)`.
- Checked state: Fill `#7A1C30` with crisp white inner marks.

### Audio Player & Scrubber Bar
- Docked or floating bottom bar with a blurred white background (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85);`).
- Progress track uses a 4px height in `#ECE5E7`, with the completed track filled in `#7A1C30` and a 14px circular thumb accented by `#9E2A42`.
- Real-time audio waveform visualizer rendered in varying opacity columns of `#7A1C30` and `#9E2A42`.
